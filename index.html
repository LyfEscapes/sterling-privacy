Sterling Solitaire

Privacy Policy · LyfEscapes LLC · Effective November 7, 2026

Sterling Solitaire is a game by LyfEscapes LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Sterling Solitaire on Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Sterling Solitaire has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no servers. We never see your name, email, contacts, photos, files or location. Tournament rivals are computer-controlled and run on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your coins, gems, cards, missions, tournament history, settings and which items you own are stored only on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your own Google account backup so it can be restored to a new phone; we cannot see that backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sterling Solitaire shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for extra undos or extra time) and an occasional full-screen ad between games. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before any ad is shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Help Center ▸ Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads.

If you buy "No Ads", the app no longer shows ads between games.

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing under the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details. The app only learns which items you bought so it can add them to your game. Coins, gems and cards have no cash value.

Sharing

The "invite" button opens Android's share sheet with a link to the game on Google Play. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

Children

Sterling Solitaire is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a future version adds online features (such as friends or clubs), this policy will be updated before those features are switched on.
